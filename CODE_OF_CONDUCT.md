# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, specific, patient, and open to correction. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Respect privacy and do not post another person’s writing, credentials, or personal information without permission.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub’s private contact options. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and protect reporters’ confidentiality where possible.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
